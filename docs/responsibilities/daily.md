# Daily Responsibilities

When you first start your day you will want to develop a routine that streamlines checking both your environment and other environments as needed.

- You will first want to start by checking your dedicated site servers to make sure there are no issues there.
- Verify Kofax license is current.
- Review emails - particularly Globalscape error emails - to be sure to catch up on what may have happened since you were last in the office.
- Join the daily SCRUM call as scheduled.
- Monitor your environment on a regular basis - Kofax services, batches moving through Kofax, release folders, etc.
- Deployments/calls as scheduled.
- Advise the team if you will be unavailable for a period of time without your phone or if you are pulled into a conflicting meeting.
- Communicate. Communicate. Communicate. - over communication with the team and operations assures everyone of what you are doing or where you are at any point during the day.
- Ask for help as needed.
    * Don't be afraid to ask for assistance. Everyone on the team is here to help you.
