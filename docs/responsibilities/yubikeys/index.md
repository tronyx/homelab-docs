# YubiKeys

## What is a YubiKey?

A YubiKey is a physical hardware security key from [Yubico](https://www.yubico.com/get-yubikey) that protects online accounts from phishing and unauthorized access by offering strong multi-factor authentication (MFA), using touch or tap to log in instead of just passwords, supporting various protocols like FIDO2/Passkeys, and being durable, waterproof, and compatible with major services like Google, Microsoft, and Apple. It acts like a digital key, making it much harder for hackers to gain access even if they steal your password, as they'd need the physical key too.


## What do we use them for?

Right now, the YubiKeys are used in conjunction with Okta for logging into the HITL (Human In The Loop) application by members of the Operations Team to perform validations.

In the near future, they will also be used in conjunction with Cisco Duo as a form of authentication for logging into any Laptop, Workstation, or Server that is part of the GovCentral Network.
