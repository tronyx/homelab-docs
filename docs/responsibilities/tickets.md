# Tickets

We are responsible for resolving tickets that get submitted to us in the two instances of Service NOW that we utilize. One is for the Corporate and FISMA side of things and the other is for the IMGS or GovCentral side of things.

Folks use these two systems to submit tickets to use for any number of things, such as account lockouts, forgotten passwords, minor software deployments, and even major outages. It is our job to ensure that they get resolved or correctly routed to the right people ASAP.

## Standard Iron Mountain SNOW

You can find the standard Iron Montain SNOW instance [HERE](https://irm.service-now.com/). It uses SSO to sign in with your Iron Mountain account and should load your default Dashboard.

## GovCentral/IMGS SNOW

You can find the IMGS/GovCentral Iron Montain SNOW instance [HERE](https://ironmountainfed.servicenowservices.com/). It uses a completely seperate account that you need to request access for. Once logged in, it should load your default Dashboard.
