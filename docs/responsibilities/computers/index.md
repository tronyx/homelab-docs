# Computers

## Corp // FISMA

Within the Corp/FISMA Network, we will help with the Workstations (PCs) whenever we can, but we are not generally responsible for them, especially now that the IRS project has been migrated to the GovCentral Network. The DLA, VHA, and Commercial projects have some Workstations on the FISMA Network that we may need to troubleshoot from time to time.

We do not handle any of the Laptops within the Corp Network. That all falls on JP, MD, and their Team.


## IMGS // GovCentral

For the IMGS/GovCentral Network, we've taken on most of the responsibility of setting up, handling the initial configuration, and troubleshooting Workstations and Laptops. John A. is still the primary contact for all things Workstations and Laptops, but we've adopted a lot of the responsibility allowing him more time to focus on the architectural and maintenance side of the house.
