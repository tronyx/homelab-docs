# Week Three

## Clearances

Each ITAR project comes with a measure of security and confidence in their respective support team members. You will often be required by a specific project to be provided a specific clearance from the Government. Project clearances can range from a simple signature on a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) to a full-on background investigation from the government, including the submission of an e-QIP (Electronic Questionnaires for Investigations Processing).

Clearance adjudication can take anywhere from a day to several months to complete. Please be mindful of providing truthful and thorough answers as there are often serious repercussions for falsifying info.

### Government Clearances at a Glance

#### Non-Sensitive / Public Trust:

- **SF-85**
- **SF-85P**

#### National Security / Confidential:

- **SF-86 / Secret**
- **Top Secret**

## Operations Observation

To completely understand the workflow of your project, no one is better to describe it to you than your local Operations personnel.

Coordinate with a manager or supervisor to shadow every aspect of what the daily workflow of Operations/Scanning entails.

Take notes, although you will never have to do their job, and gain the perspective of how each part of the Operations workflow contributes to your work.
