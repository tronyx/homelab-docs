# Getting Started

Our projects fall into 3 categories:

1. Commercial customers handling ITAR-protected data
2. Government entities
3. Standalone/offline sites

We use a FISMA environment as the central hub of our operations. Only ITAR personnel can connect to our servers and infrastructure. Due to this we are required to have additional background checks as needed and also be US citizens.

Everyone is issued a company-provided laptop and smartphone to perform their daily duties.

Your first day will be comprised of [onboarding](https://irm-eit.gitlab.io/irm-mkdocs/team/week-1/#onboarding) with the Iron Mountain HR. Your second day will likely be at your project site. Further details will be explained in later modules following your [Training Schedule](week-1.md).
