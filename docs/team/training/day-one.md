## Your (Second) First Day

Your first day on site, really you're your second day, you will meet your Team and the Operations personnel you support. You should have the following items with you, if you are working on site:

- **Company provided:** Laptop, smartphone
- **Your own:** Backpack, headset, notebook

If you need any of the items above (even ones that you are expected to provide) please reach out to your Hiring Manager for assistance.
