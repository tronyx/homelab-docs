# Week One

## Onboarding

Onboarding will be handled by Human Resources and is a work-from-home day. Be sure to have your laptop connected to the internet and your company phone turned on.

Tasks completed during onboarding:

1. Review and verify employee info
2. Connect to the VPN
3. Complete HR documentation and benefits enrollment


## Team Goals

These are the DMS ITAR Team expectations:

1. Your first goal or focus is to assist your primary site(the one you were hired on to manage) with whatever is needed. Whether it's deploying new batch classes for implementations or moving desks and setting up workstations, it's your duty to work with local operations and implementations to help with the success of the project. If you are unsure about anything, never be afraid to speak up and ask for advice or assistance. If any conflicts arise between meetings or anything else, speak up so others can help! 
2. Once a project has successfully kicked off and deployments are complete, you'll transition into monitoring the site and keeping an eye on different processes. The goal is to catch issues before Operations. 
3. You'll be asked to assist in other ITAR-related projects. One of our largest shared tasks is monitoring our "Kofax Central" shared environment. This environment is shared between our commercial and a few government-level projects that don't require the same level of clearance as those placed within our "FISMA" environment. 


## Access

You will need to request access to certain applications and repositories for your daily use.

- **ADM account creation** - Specific account for RDP access into Workstations and Servers.
- **One Identity Access (AD)** - Active Directory.
- **Adding ADM account to AD groups** - Specific ITAR groups for access to servers and workstations within our environments.
- **Local Laptop ADMIN access** - For installing required applications.
- **GitLab Access** - Used as a file repository by the configuration team for Kofax or other type of deployments.
- **Kofax KB and ticket system** - Kofax vendor site for opening support cases and referencing their support knowledge base.


## Laptop Setup

If you're already cleared for your project, you will need a few of the following items installed on your laptop for your daily duties.


### Windows Laptop

- **mRemote NG** - You will need administrator access to install, but this program allows you to remotely access servers and workstations within our environment.
- **Putty (Self Service Client)** - Putty is needed for SSH sessions into certain Linux servers within our environment.
- **7-zip (Self Service Client)** - 7-zip is an application that is used to open compressed .zip, 7z, .rar archive files.
- **Notepad++ (Self Service Client)** - Notepad++ is a great alternative to Windows Notepad, this is used daily by members of the team for editing XML, CSV, and other files.
- **Google File Stream / Google Drive (Self Service Client)** - Google Drive File Stream is great for syncing local folders with your Google Drive.

### MacBook

- **Windows App (Self Service Client)** - Allows you to connect to the Jump Server via RDP.
- **Visual Studio Code (Self Service Client)** - Great overall IDE (Code Editor).
