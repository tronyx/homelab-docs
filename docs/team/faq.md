# FAQ

## What is FISMA?

FISMA 2014 codifies the Department of Homeland Security's role in administering the implementation of information security policies for federal Executive Branch civilian agencies, overseeing agencies' compliance with those policies, and assisting OMB in developing those policies.  (from CISA.gov) We refer to the central hub of our environment as FISMA. Most, if not all, of the ITAR projects will use our FISMA environment.


## What is ITAR?

International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) establishes controls regarding the export and import of defense-related items and services that appear on the United States Munitions List (USML). ITAR is meant to limit access to specific technologies and their associated data resources.

## How do I request time off?

Navigate to the [myMAP](https://irm.service-now.com/mymap) application and then choose 'Kronos'. From this application, you can view your remaining time-off balance and request time off on the calendar. Please be sure to notify your manager/lead as soon as you know when you will be taking time off. After you have submitted your request, also be sure to update the [Service OPS Vacation and On Call calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=ironmountain.com_lkgnb6m0gckslfr0b709gaj63o%40group.calendar.google.com&ctz=America%2FNew_York) as well as the [Team Coverage Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1itrkrPa_ZypsAqq9PMGHNNu7wEACkVU87rDJEBN27xw/edit?hl=en&forcehl=1#gid=1629222378).


## When will I be cleared for my project?

There is no 'easy' answer to this question as background investigations (BI) can take anywhere for a few weeks to a few months, with a possible interview with an NSA agent. Patience is key here. There are also site contacts that can provide some insight into your clearance process. Please reach out to your manager/lead for the correct contact to use.
