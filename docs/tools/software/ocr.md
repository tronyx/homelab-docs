# Optical Character Recognition (OCR)

The technology that enables computers to read and understand text in images. It is used to convert scanned documents, PDFs, and other images of text into machine-readable text that can be edited, searched, and stored.

Document OCR is a specific type of OCR that is used to convert scanned documents, such as contracts, invoices, and receipts, into text. Document OCR software typically includes features for correcting errors, extracting tables and forms, and translating text into other languages.


## Features

The main features of document OCR include:

- **Text recognition:** The ability to recognize text in a variety of fonts, sizes, and styles.
- **Error correction:** The ability to correct errors in the scanned document, such as smudges, shadows, and poor contrast.
- **Form extraction:** The ability to extract text from forms and tables.
- **Language translation:** The ability to translate text into other languages.


## Components

The components of a document OCR system include:

- **Scanner:** A device that converts a physical document into a digital image.
- **OCR software:** The software that performs the text recognition and other tasks.
- **Text editor:** A software application for editing and formatting text.


## Industries

Document OCR is used in a variety of industries, including:

- **Legal:** To convert scanned documents into electronic files that can be easily searched and stored.
- **Finance:** To extract data from financial documents, such as invoices and bank statements.
- **Healthcare:** To convert medical records into electronic form for easier access and storage.
- **Government:** To digitize government records, such as passports and driver's licenses.
- **Business:** To convert business documents, such as contracts and invoices, into electronic form for easier collaboration and management.


## Accuracy

The accuracy of document OCR depends on a number of factors, including the quality of the scanned document, the complexity of the text, and the capabilities of the OCR software. In general, document OCR can achieve a high degree of accuracy, but it is important to test the software with a variety of documents to ensure that it meets your specific needs.


## Benefits

Here are some of the benefits of using document OCR:

- **Reduces manual data entry:** OCR can automate the process of data entry, which can save time and money.
- **Improves data accuracy:** OCR can help to improve the accuracy of data entry by reducing the number of errors.
- **Makes documents more accessible:** OCR can make documents more accessible to people with disabilities, such as blindness or low vision.
- **Enables data mining:** OCR can be used to mine data from documents, which can be used for a variety of purposes, such as market research and customer analysis.
