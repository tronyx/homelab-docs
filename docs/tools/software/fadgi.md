# FADGI Compliance

FADGI stands for Federal Agencies Digital Guidelines Initiative. It is a collaborative effort started in 2007 by federal agencies to articulate common sustainable practices and guidelines for digitized and born digital historical, archival and cultural content.

FADGI compliance is the act of following the FADGI guidelines when digitizing or converting analog content to digital format. The guidelines cover a wide range of topics, including image quality, metadata, file formats, and preservation.

You can check out the official site [HERE](https://www.digitizationguidelines.gov/).


## Guidelines

The FADGI guidelines are divided into three levels of compliance:

- **1-star:** Basic level of compliance that ensures that the digitized content is readable and navigable.
- **2-star:** Intermediate level of compliance that ensures that the digitized content is of high quality and can be used for research and other purposes.
- **3-star:** Highest level of compliance that ensures that the digitized content is of archival quality and will be preserved for future generations.


## 3-Star Requirements

The 3-star requirements for FADGI compliance are as follows:

- **Image resolution:** 300 ppi
- **Color depth:** 24 bits
- **File format:** TIFF or JPEG2000
- **Metadata:** Dublin Core or EAD
- **Preservation:** Long-term storage and access

FADGI compliance is important for ensuring the quality and longevity of digitized content. By following the FADGI guidelines, organizations can create digital records that are accurate, readable, and accessible for future generations.


## Benefits

Here are some of the benefits of FADGI compliance:

- **Improved image quality:** FADGI guidelines ensure that digitized images are of high quality and can be used for research and other purposes.
- **Increased longevity:** FADGI guidelines help to ensure that digitized content is preserved for future generations.
- **Reduced costs:** FADGI compliance can help to reduce the cost of digitization by avoiding the need to re-digitize content that does not meet the guidelines.
- **Improved efficiency:** FADGI guidelines can help to streamline the digitization process and make it more efficient.
