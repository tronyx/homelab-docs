# Kodak

![Kodak](../../overrides/assets/images/kodak.png#center)

Kodak i5000 Series Scanners have been the choice of the most demanding customers for the toughest high volume scanning environments – service bureaus, business process outsourcing organizations, and corporate scanning departments. The i5000 Series offer efficiency and reliability that can positively impact productivity and enable true, high-volume document processing.


## Features

- **High-Speed Performance:** Up to 210 ppm (pages per minute) / 420 ipm (images per minute) for duplex scanning.
- **High-Volume Ready:** Designed for unlimited daily scanning volume.
- **Advanced Image Processing:** Kodak Perfect Page Technology, automatic cropping, deskew, color dropout, and quality enhancements.
- **Intelligent Document Protection (IDP):** Detects stapled documents and stops the feed to prevent damage.
- **Versatile Feeder:** 750-sheet capacity, handles various paper weights (45-200 gsm) and sizes, including long documents up to 4.6m.
- **Smart Features:** Barcode reading, image addressing for patch codes, multi-feed detection, and controlled stacking.
- **Connectivity:** USB 2.0 Certified, USB 3.0 compatible, supports TWAIN, ISIS, and WIA drivers.
- **Operator Control:** Includes an Interactive Operator Control Panel (OCP) and a touch screen for easy management.
- **Output Flexibility:** Options for rear exit tray, dual-stream scanning, and an optional printer for post-scan imprinting.


## Applications

The Kodak i5850 is a high-volume production scanner designed for organizations that process vast quantities of diverse documents. Its key application examples involve managing and automating large-scale information capture in demanding environments, with features like intelligent sorting and high-speed processing.

### Document Management & Archival

**Digital Mailrooms:** Efficiently processing and digitizing incoming physical mail for digital distribution and archival.
**High-Volume Archival:** Scanning large quantities of documents to create FADGI-compliant archival-quality images for long-term preservation.
**Legacy Document Conversion:** Converting massive backlogs of physical records into searchable digital formats.


### Business Process Automation

**Financial Services:** Processing loan applications, claims, and other financial paperwork for multinational firms.
**Insurance Services:** Handling large volumes of policy documents and claims forms.
**Government Entities:** Managing public records, applications, and official documentation at scale.
**Manufacturing Operations:** Digitizing extensive manufacturing records, quality control documents, and inventory lists.


### Advanced Sorting & Data Capture

**Exception Handling:** Using features like the optional "S" model's sorting capabilities to automatically separate multi-feeds (e.g., documents with sticky notes) or different document types to different output trays, minimizing manual intervention.
**Intelligent Data Entry:** Utilizing patch code and barcode reading to automatically sort documents, apply sequential numbering (image addressing), and track batches accurately. This data can then be used to streamline data entry workflows.
**Automated Accounts Payable:** Integrating with solutions like KODAK Info Input to automate accounts payable processes by intelligently processing invoices and related documents.
