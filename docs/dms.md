## What's the point?

The goal of DMS (Digital Media Solutions) is taking physical media from a customer and scanning/organizing it into a digital format.

To achieve this, our Operations personnel prepare the physical media (paper, microfilm, blueprints, etc) and then the media is scanned. Scanners can range from a desktop scanner to a large-format flatbed scanner (and everything in-between).

After the physical media has been scanned into our servers, the images are ingested into Kofax Capture. A more in-depth analysis of Kofax Capture will be covered next week, but a brief explanation is that Kofax takes the images scanned by Operations and organizes the images. After the images are organized, they are then released from Kofax in a compressed format. The output files are then delivered to the customer either digitally through a secure connection or securely encrypted and stored on hard-drive.

Operations personnel are required to meet certain expectations for the customer and deliver by a pre-determined turnaround time.

Our role is to make sure that Operations' hard work is not in vain.
